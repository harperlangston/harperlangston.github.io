DGESDD computes the singular value decomposition (SVD) of a real M-by-N matrix A, optionally computing the left and right singular vectors. If singular vectors are desired, it uses a divide-and-conquer algorithm.

The SVD is written

A = U $\ast$ SIGMA $\ast$ transpose(V)

where SIGMA is an M-by-N matrix which is zero except for its min(m,n) diagonal elements, U is an M-by-M orthogonal matrix, and V is an N-by-N orthogonal matrix. The diagonal elements of SIGMA ` $\ast$ are the singular values of A; they are real and non-negative, and are returned in descending order. The first min(m,n) columns of U and V are the left and right singular vectors of A.

DGESVD computes the singular value decomposition (SVD) of a real M-by-N matrix A, optionally computing the left and/or right singular vectors. The SVD is written

A = U $\ast$ SIGMA $\ast$ transpose(V)

where SIGMA is an M-by-N matrix which is zero except for its min(m,n) diagonal elements, U is an M-by-M orthogonal matrix, and V is an N-by-N orthogonal matrix. The diagonal elements of SIGMA are the singular values of A; they are real and non-negative, and are returned in descending order. The first min(m,n) columns of U and V are the left and right singular vectors of A.

DGETRF computes an LU factorization of a general M-by-N matrix A using partial pivoting with row interchanges.

The factorization has the form

A = P $\ast$ L $\ast$ U

where P is a permutation matrix, L is lower triangular with unit diagonal elements (lower trapezoidal if m $>$ n), and U is upper triangular (upper trapezoidal if m $<$ n).

This method inverts U and then computes inv(A) by solving the system inv(A)$\ast$L = inv(U) for inv(A).
